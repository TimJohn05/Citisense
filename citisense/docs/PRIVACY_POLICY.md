# CitiSense Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

## 1. Overview
CitiSense is a barangay feedback and resolution platform for Calamba City,
Laguna. This policy explains how the app handles your personal data when you
use it to file a community report.

## 2. Permissions we request
- **Camera** — used only to capture an evidence photo when you file a report
  and a proof photo when an official resolves it. Photos are captured only when
  you tap the capture button.
- **Location** — used only to pin the exact location of the issue you are
  reporting on the map. You can drag the map to choose the pin manually, or
  press the crosshair button to use your device's current GPS position. Your
  location is never tracked in the background.

## 3. What we collect
When you file a report we store: your account name and email, the report
description and category, the map coordinates of the pin, the evidence photo
you upload, and the resolution status. Barangay officials and system
administrators can view reports to act on them.

## 4. How we use it
Data is used only to receive, route and resolve community complaints: showing
officials where the issue is, attaching visual evidence, and keeping a public
audit trail of how reports were handled.

## 5. Where it is stored
In the demonstration build, reports are stored only on your device. When the
live backend is enabled, reports are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and
Storage), whose servers may be located outside the Philippines.

## 6. Sharing
We do not sell or rent your personal data. Report information is visible to
barangay officials and administrators of the system for the purpose of
resolving reports.

## 7. Your choices
You may decline any permission at any time. Without the camera and location
permissions you can still read reports, but you cannot fully file a new
report. You may sign out or delete the app at any time; reports you filed may
remain on the backend for the barangay's records.

## 8. Contact
For privacy questions, contact the barangay administrator through the local
barangay hall or the project team behind CitiSense.
